# SEO fix: META_DESC_MISSING

**Page:** https://ai-newsroom-v1uc.vercel.app/

**Problem:** Page has no meta description.

**Why:** The page lacks a meta description, causing search engines to display irrelevant snippets or none, hurting click‑through rates. Adding a concise, keyword‑rich description improves visibility and encourages users to visit. The proposed description highlights AI news and invites engagement.

**Proposed value:** Stay ahead with the latest AI breakthroughs, trends, and expert insights. Explore our curated news and join the future of technology.

```html
<meta name="description" content="Stay ahead with the latest AI breakthroughs, trends, and expert insights. Explore our curated news and join the future of technology.">
```
