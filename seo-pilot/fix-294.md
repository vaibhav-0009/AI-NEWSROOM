# SEO fix: H1_MISSING

**Page:** https://ai-newsroom-v1uc.vercel.app/

**Problem:** Page has no H1 heading.

**Why:** The page lacks an H1 tag, which signals to search engines the main topic of the page and aids screen readers. Without it, rankings can suffer and users may not understand the page’s purpose at a glance. Adding a concise H1 clarifies content intent and improves accessibility.

**Proposed value:** AI Newsroom: Latest Updates on Artificial Intelligence

```html
<h1>AI Newsroom: Latest Updates on Artificial Intelligence</h1>
```
